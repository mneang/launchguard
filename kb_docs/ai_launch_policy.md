# Internal AI Launch Readiness Policy
Synthetic document for demo use only.

An internal AI feature team may be considered launch-ready only when all required roles meet certification readiness requirements, complete responsible AI training, and pass the readiness assessment threshold.

Minimum readiness requirements:
- AI Engineer: AI-102 and Responsible AI Foundations.
- Platform Engineer: AZ-204 and Responsible AI Foundations.
- Product Lead: Responsible AI Foundations.

Launch approval should not be granted when:
- Any required role is missing a required certification.
- Any required role has a practice score below 75%.
- Any required role has fewer than 15 focus hours available across the next two weeks.
- Any readiness evidence cannot be traced to approved learning or policy material.

Managers must review the final readiness memo before approving launch.
