# Workload and Learning Capacity Guidelines
Synthetic document for demo use only.

Meeting-heavy schedules reduce realistic certification progress.

Guidelines:
- More than 20 meeting hours per week indicates high workload.
- Fewer than 10 focus hours per week indicates capacity risk.
- 12 to 18 meeting hours and at least 15 focus hours is considered healthy for certification progress.
- Study plans should protect focus-heavy periods and avoid unrealistic daily commitments.
